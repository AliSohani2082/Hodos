## Question

What are we deciding, in one or two sentences? For a spike, copy the go/no-go question from its README.

## Context

What forces the decision: the requirement it serves, the constraints (security, cost, stack), and what we knew before the spike. Link the PRD sections and research reports instead of restating them.

## Verdict

**Go · Go with changes · No-go** on *the thing being decided*, in one sentence.

State the decision in the active voice: "We will use X for Y."

## Evidence

What the spike measured, with enough detail to repeat it: the setup, the numbers, the exact versions and flags. Link to the code, fixtures and raw results under `spikes/`. State plainly what was not tested.

## Consequences

What changes in the PRD, the architecture or the plan. Say what gets easier and what gets harder. If this amends the PRD, update the PRD in the same change and list the sections here.
